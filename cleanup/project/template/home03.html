About Us

Vacate Cleaning Solutions Pros started in 2011 as a small domestic cleaning service company. Since then, we have broadened our services to include a wide array of cleaning solutions, such as end of lease cleaning, window cleaning, steam cleaning, pressure cleaning, and tile and grout cleaning across the Perth region. Our dedication and consistent hard work have enabled us to secure relationships with over 50 real estates, who now recommend us as their preferred cleaners in Perth. Our journey reflects our commitment to quality and our ongoing efforts to exceed our clients’ expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to urgent requests?

For urgent re-clean requests within our 72-hour guarantee window, we aim to schedule service within 24 hours.

Do you provide written quotes before booking?

Yes. Every quote is provided in writing via email with full scope of work and pricing breakdown before any payment.

Can I modify my booking after confirmation?

Yes. Contact us at least 48 hours before your scheduled service to modify date, time, or scope without penalties.
